# Phase 06.1 Plan 01: Grant Initial AI Credits at Pro Checkout Summary

**New `grantInitialCredits` internal mutation grants 100 AI credits with reset-to-100 (non-additive) semantics on subscription-mode Stripe checkout, wired alongside `upsertSubscription` with a collision-safe `:credits-grant` idempotency key that closes the v1.0 milestone-audit BLOCKER (new Pro subscribers previously received zero credits until their first monthly renewal).**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~20 min
- **Completed:** 2026-07-12
- **Tasks:** 2/2 completed
- **Files modified:** 4

## Accomplishments

- New Pro subscriber now receives exactly 100 AI credits the moment `checkout.session.completed` (subscription mode) fires, instead of hitting `INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS` on their first AI action
- Closed the idempotency-key-collision landmine flagged in `06.1-CONTEXT.md`: the grant uses a `${stripeEventId}:credits-grant` key, verified by regression tests at both the unit (`aiCredits.test.ts`) and dispatcher (`stripeWebhooks.test.ts`) levels to never collide with `upsertSubscription`'s raw-key `processedStripeEvents` row
- Re-subscription resets a leftover balance to exactly 100 (D-01), never stacks additively (42 → 100, not 142)
- Full existing test suite (100/100) remains green — no regression to subscription creation, anomaly handling, or any other webhook path

- `convex/stripeWebhooks.test.ts` - Inverted the line-176 test (balance now asserted as 100, type `"reset"`, no `"topup"` row), added a dispatcher-level replay landmine test and a D-01 re-subscription test (42 → 100)

## Decisions Made

- Reused the existing `"reset"` `creditTransactions.type` literal for the initial grant rather than adding a new type, per D-07 and to keep the transaction history semantically consistent with "balance was set to the full quota" (matches `resetCredits`)
- Grant call ordered after `upsertSubscription` in the subscription branch (subscription row exists before credits are granted) — correctness does not depend on this order since the two mutations' idempotency keys never collide, but this ordering is the more intuitive read of "upsert subscription, then grant its benefit"
